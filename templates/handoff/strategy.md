## Task: which roles should this candidate target, and why?

Act as a career strategist. Using FACTS only:

1. Write a five-line candidate frame: seniority and years, core stack, languages,
   where they can legally work, pay floor. Cite the FACTS line each one came from.
2. List 5–8 role families worth pursuing. Respect the tiers in FACTS: T1 is the
   career target and outranks everything; T2 is adjacent and must never rank above
   an equally good T1 role; T3 is a stopgap and only worth it when its pay beats the
   candidate's current job.
3. For each family give: **why it fits** (quote the FACTS line, verbatim),
   **reachable?** (can they take it from where they live, under the location rules in
   FACTS), **money** (does it plausibly clear the floor; say "unverified" for any
   market figure, since you cannot search), and **main risk** (what a hiring manager
   would probe).
4. Finish with: the 2–3 families to spend the next two weeks on, the families to
   deliberately skip and why, and three concrete next moves.

Drop any family that breaks a deal-breaker or hard disqualifier in FACTS.
